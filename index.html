Constituency Netball Championship 2025

Welcome to the official website for the Constituency Netball Championship, uniting villages across Namungalwe, Nabitende, Nambale, Kidaago, and Nawandala sub-counties.

Village Knockouts

September 6–October 12, 2025: Village teams compete to become one of 24 parish champions.

Parish Knockouts

October 18–November 9, 2025: Parish champions vie for 5 sub-county titles.

Sub-County League

December 27, 2025–March 1, 2026: Sub-county champions play a home-and-away league to crown the constituency champion.
